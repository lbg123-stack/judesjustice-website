PMLD
Profound and multiple learning disabilities. People with PMLD often have complex health needs, sensory impairments and no speech. They depend on others for everything, so when a system fails them, it fails the whole family.

Did you know?

Things many families only find out years later.

  • From 16, a young person can claim benefits in their own right.
  • A doctor's form can unlock the highest rate of Adult Disability Payment, without a points assessment.
  • The mobility part of disability benefits can pay for a wheelchair accessible vehicle.
  • Equipment such as hoists and beds can be delivered to your holiday accommodation.
  • Hydrotherapy can ease pain and stiffness. Ask the physiotherapist.
  • Free specialist ketogenic dietitians exist for drug-resistant epilepsy.
  • Families can pool their self-directed support budgets.
  • Carers can ask for their own support plan.

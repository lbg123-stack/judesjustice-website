Independent advice

Some organisations are funded by the council. They can still help you, but if you want to challenge a council decision, you may prefer advice from an organisation that is independent of the council, such as a law centre or independent advocacy.

Legal help

These organisations can give legal advice or help you find a lawyer. Jude's Justice gives information, not legal advice.

Govan Law Centre

Glasgow

A law centre giving free legal advice and representation, including on public law and challenges to decisions by councils.
